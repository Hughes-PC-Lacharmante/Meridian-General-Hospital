Known Patient Module Issues

Identified during MLHMS legacy assessment

Direct identifier-based lookup The original application relies heavily on the patient identifier supplied by the user. Record-level authorization must not depend solely on hiding links in the interface.
Fragmented clinical data Patient information is distributed across separate patient, laboratory, pharmacy and billing modules.
Incomplete access audit Historical records may capture that a record was viewed without adequately recording why, what was viewed or the full actor context.
Legacy extensions use different controls Laboratory and reporting extensions may not interpret authorization rules in the same way as the original patient module.
