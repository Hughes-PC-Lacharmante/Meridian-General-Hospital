Legacy Employee Lifecycle

Expected HR workflow versus actual legacy synchronization

1. Hire HR creates employee record and informs IT.
2. Account Creation AD account is created, then MLHMS and departmental accounts may be created separately.
3. Department Change HR updates employee details, but application permissions may not be updated automatically.
4. Termination HR marks the employee as terminated and IT disables AD.
5. Legacy Cleanup Local MLHMS and extension accounts may remain active because there is no centralized deprovisioning process.

HR / Identity Security Issues

Findings identified during the MLHMS legacy assessment.

Terminated employee account remains active HR termination does not automatically revoke every MLHMS and departmental credential.
Manual provisioning New employees require multiple manual account creation steps across different systems.
Department changes may not update privileges An employee transferring from one department may retain permissions belonging to the old role.
No central identity lifecycle HR, Active Directory, MLHMS and extensions effectively maintain separate representations of the same employee.
Ownership and review responsibilities unclear It is not always clear whether HR, IT or the individual application owner is responsible for removing legacy access.
