Staff Management Security Issues

Legacy findings associated with workforce administration.

Duplicate employee records Staff records can differ between HR and the local MLHMS staff register.
Permission drift Department or position changes can leave old privileges assigned to employees.
Local role interpretation The Staff module may interpret USER, USER+ and ADMIN differently from other MLHMS modules.
Manual approval workflow Privilege changes often require manual communication between HR, IT and department managers.
No centralized role authority There is no single authoritative source defining which application permissions belong to each staff role.
