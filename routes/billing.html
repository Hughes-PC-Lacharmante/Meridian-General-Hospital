Known Billing Module Issues

Findings identified in the legacy environment

Separate billing identity store Billing accounts can exist independently of the primary hospital identity system.
Manual patient-ID reconciliation Billing identifiers are sometimes reconciled manually with identifiers from the patient system.
Broad billing access The legacy USER role can provide access to billing functions beyond what some departments require.
Weak change traceability Some invoice changes record the last user but not the complete previous/new state or business reason.
Financial and clinical data are not centrally correlated Patient information and financial information are maintained in separate legacy workflows.
